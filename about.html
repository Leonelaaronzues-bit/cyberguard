About CyberGuard

Learn more about the project and its mission.

🛡️ What is CyberGuard?

CyberGuard is a personal cybersecurity awareness project designed to make digital security easier to understand and more accessible.

🎯 Our Mission

The goal is to provide simple security tools, practical advice and educational information that can help users develop safer digital habits.

🔐 Why Cybersecurity Matters

Our phones, computers, accounts and personal information are an important part of our daily lives. Learning how to protect them is an essential digital skill.
