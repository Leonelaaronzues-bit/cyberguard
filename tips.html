🛡️ Security Tips

Simple habits that can help you stay safer online.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using easily guessed personal information.

🔑 Enable Two-Factor Authentication

When available, enable two-factor authentication to add an extra layer of protection to your accounts.

🎣 Watch Out for Phishing

Be careful with unexpected messages, suspicious links and requests for passwords or personal information.

📱 Keep Your Devices Updated

Keep your operating system and applications updated so you receive important security fixes.

🌐 Think Before You Click

Check links and websites carefully before entering passwords or other sensitive information.
